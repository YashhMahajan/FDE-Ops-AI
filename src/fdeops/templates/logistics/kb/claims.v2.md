## Damage claims {#damage-claims}

You can file a damage claim in the 30 days after delivery. The claim amount cannot be more than
the declared value of the shipment.

## Large claims {#large-claims}

Claims above $300.00 must have the approval of a claims specialist. Support sends these claims to
the claims team.

## Loss claims {#loss-claims}

A loss claim is possible when the shipment status is "lost".
