## Shipment statuses {#statuses}

Each shipment has a status. "Label created" means that we have the shipment data. "In transit"
means that the parcel moves through our network. "Out for delivery" means that a driver has the
parcel today. "Delivered" means that the recipient has the parcel.

## Expected delivery date {#eta}

The expected delivery date (ETA) shows on the tracking page. We calculate it when the parcel
starts to move.
