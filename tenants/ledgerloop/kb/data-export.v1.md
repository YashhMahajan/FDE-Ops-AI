## Who can request an export {#who-can-request}

Only workspace Owners and Admins can request a full data export. A Member must tell an Owner or an
Admin of the workspace to make the request.

## Delivery {#delivery}

We prepare the export in the background. We send it by email to the requester in 24 hours, as a
ZIP archive of CSV files.
