## Who can request an export {#who-can-request}

Only workspace Owners and Admins can request a full data export. Members should ask an Owner or
Admin of their workspace to make the request.

## Delivery {#delivery}

Exports are prepared in the background and emailed to the requester within 24 hours as a ZIP
archive of CSV files.
