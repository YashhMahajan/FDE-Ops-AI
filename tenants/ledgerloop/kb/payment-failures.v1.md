## Automatic retries {#retry-schedule}

If a payment fails, we retry the charge automatically 3 times over 7 days. Your workspace stays
active during the retry period. If all retries fail, the account is marked past due.

## Updating your card {#update-card}

Workspace Owners and Admins can update the card under Settings > Billing > Payment method. Once the
card is updated, the open invoice is retried within one hour.
