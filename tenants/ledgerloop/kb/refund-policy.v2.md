## Refund window {#refund-window}

You can request a refund of a paid invoice within 14 days of the payment date. After 14 days,
invoices are no longer eligible for a refund, but you can cancel or downgrade to avoid future
charges.

## Large refunds {#large-refunds}

Refunds above $200.00 are reviewed by a billing specialist before they are issued. Support will
pass your request to the billing team, who reply within two business days.

## How refunds are paid {#payout}

Refunds go back to the original payment method. Depending on your bank, they appear within 5 to 10
business days.
