## Refund window {#refund-window}

You can request a refund of a paid invoice in the 14 days after the payment date. After 14 days, the
invoice cannot get a refund. To stop future charges, you can cancel your plan or change to a
smaller plan.

## Large refunds {#large-refunds}

Refunds above $200.00 must have the approval of a billing specialist before we send them. Support
sends your request to the billing team. The billing team replies in two business days.

## How refunds are paid {#payout}

We send refunds to the payment method that you used for the invoice. The refund can need 5 to 10
business days to show on your bank statement.
